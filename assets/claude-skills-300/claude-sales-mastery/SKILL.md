The Methodology
This system is built on proven sales frameworks:
The Three 10s Framework
Every sale requires the prospect to reach a 10 (on a 1-10 scale) on three elements:
1. The Product — They believe it's the best solution
1. You — They trust you personally
1. The Company — They trust the organization behind it
Most salespeople only pitch the product (element 1) and wonder why they can't close. The system builds all three.
The 10 Core Tonalities
How you say something matters 4.5x more than what you say. The system teaches and applies:
1. Scarcity (whisper with power)
1. Absolute Certainty (rock-solid conviction)
1. Reasonable Man ("sound fair enough?")
1. Declarative as Question (triggers brain's search mode)
1. I Care (warm, genuine)
1. I Really Want to Know (deep curiosity)
1. Implied Obviousness ("of course")
1. Utter Sincerity (heartfelt, not pushy)
1. Money-Aside (removes pressure)
1. Mystery & Intrigue (drawing them in)
The Safe-Cracker Model
Think of closing as cracking a safe with 5 numbers:
1. Product certainty
1. Trust in you
1. Trust in company
1. Low action threshold (risk feels small)
1. High pain threshold (cost of inaction feels massive)
Miss one number, the safe doesn't open. The system ensures you hit all five.
The Looping Method
When a prospect objects, most salespeople either give up or push harder. Both fail. The Looping Method is different:
Acknowledge the objection (don't fight it)  Identify which of the Three 10s is weak
Loop back with a new pattern that builds THAT specific certainty
Lower the action threshold (guarantees, trials, risk reversal)  Raise the pain threshold (what happens if they DON'T act?)  Ask for the order again
You can loop 2-3 times before it becomes pushy. The system gives you distinct patterns for each loop.
